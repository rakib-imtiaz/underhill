      1. The survey drone. An enterprise survey quadcopter that maps ground from the air: a square fuselage with hot-swap batteries, four folding carbon arms with brushless motors and folding propellers, two splayed landing legs, a gimbal camera underneath and a second camera on top.
      2. The GNSS base station. An antenna dome on a range pole, a radio with a whip antenna, and a tripod head on three carbon legs with flip-lock clamps and steel spikes.
      3. The total station. A telescope with an EDM rangefinder and objective lens, a carry handle, display and keypad, a tribrach with levelling screws, and a tripod.
      4. In the field. On a valley site at dusk the three work together: the base station fixes a control mark, the total station measures a prism on the far slope, and the drone scans a staked parcel into a point cloud that resolves into contours.
      5. The bigger picture. Pulled back to the planet, the valley is one point in a global frame, tied to it by the positioning satellites overhead.
